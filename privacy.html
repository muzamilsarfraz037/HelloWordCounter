Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how Word Counter handles information when you use our website and its text-counting tools.

1. Information We Collect

Word Counter is designed to provide a simple text-counting service. The text you enter into the Word Counter tool is processed directly in your browser and is not intentionally stored on our server.

We do not require you to create an account or provide personal information to use the basic word-counting features of the website.

2. How We Use Information

Any information that may be collected through normal website operation may be used to maintain, improve, secure and understand the performance and usability of our website.

3. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies now or in the future. These technologies may be used to support website functionality, understand website usage and, where applicable, support advertising services.

You can manage or disable cookies through the settings of your web browser. Disabling certain cookies may affect some website functionality.

4. Advertising

We may display advertisements on this website through third-party advertising services such as Google AdSense. These services may use cookies or similar technologies to provide, personalize and measure advertisements.

Third-party advertising providers may collect information about users' visits to websites in accordance with their own privacy policies and applicable laws.

5. Third-Party Services

Our website may use third-party services for advertising, analytics, security or other website functionality. These third parties may collect or process information according to their own privacy policies.

We recommend reviewing the privacy policies of any third-party services that may be used on this website.

6. Children's Privacy

Our website is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children. We encourage parents and guardians to supervise children's use of online services.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to maintain the security and reliability of our website and to protect information that may be handled through normal website operation.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to our website, services or applicable requirements. Any updates will be published on this page.

9. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, you can contact us through our Contact Us page.
